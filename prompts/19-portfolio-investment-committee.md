# 19. Portfolio investment committee

Judge every project as if you had to decide where to put real money.

**Copy-paste:**

```
Act as an investment committee with 1 million dollars to place across my
projects and businesses. For each one, collect evidence from my repos, logs,
analytics and revenue records: traction, revenue, defensibility, market size,
distribution advantage, time it takes from me, and opportunity cost.

Score each project with that evidence, citing the file or URL for every number.
Mark anything you could not verify.

Return a ranked list with one verdict each: scale, maintain, pivot, or shut down,
with the one reason that decides it. End with the single change in where my time
goes that this ranking implies.
```

**Notes**
- The verdict is only as good as the evidence. Ask for sources on every number.
- Expect it to say "shut down" to something you like. That is the point.

**Origin**
- From an AI assistant's answer to "how should I spend a large budget on my strongest
  model?" (November 2026). The ranking in that answer was its own guess.
- Limit: not yet run and measured by this repo. Open a PR with your result.
