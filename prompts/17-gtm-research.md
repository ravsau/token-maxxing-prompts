# 17. Go-to-market research

Give distribution the same engineering effort as the product.

**Copy-paste:**

```
For [BUSINESS], research who would pay for it and how to reach them.

1. Describe the buyer: role, company size, the problem they search for, budget.
2. Find where they gather: forums, subreddits, newsletters, communities, events,
   search queries. Give links.
3. Study how 5 competitors acquire customers: channels, offers, pricing, content.
4. Find one underserved segment competitors ignore, with evidence.
5. Build a prospect list of 25 named people or companies that fit, each with the
   public source that shows the fit.
6. Draft a short, personal first message for the top 10. Draft only.

Do not send anything. Every claim needs a link.
```

**Notes**
- The prospect list with sources is the durable output. Messages go stale fast.
- Sending is the bottleneck, not finding. Plan the send before you run this.

**Origin**
- From an AI assistant's answer to "how should I spend a large budget on my strongest
  model?" (November 2026). The ranking in that answer was its own guess.
- Limit: not yet run and measured by this repo. Open a PR with your result.
