# 18. Video back-catalog optimization

Find old videos worth reviving and set up experiments with evidence behind them.

**Copy-paste:**

```
Go through my whole video catalog: titles, descriptions, transcripts, search
terms, retention curves, views over time, and where viewers go next.

Find the evergreen videos: steady search traffic, good retention, weak packaging
or no call to action. For each, say what is wrong and what to test (title,
thumbnail, description, chapters, end screen, pinned comment, link).

Rank the top 15 by expected gain. For each, write the experiment: what changes,
the metric, the baseline, and how long to run it. Prepare the new metadata as
drafts. Do not change anything live.
```

**Notes**
- Use a local copy of your channel data if you have one. API quota runs out fast.
- One change per video per test, or you cannot tell what worked.

**Origin**
- From an AI assistant's answer to "how should I spend a large budget on my strongest
  model?" (November 2026). The ranking in that answer was its own guess.
- Limit: not yet run and measured by this repo. Open a PR with your result.
