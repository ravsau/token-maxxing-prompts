# 14. Best-in-class product challenge

Pick one product and make it the best at one specific thing. The goal is a clear
win on one dimension, not feature parity.

**Copy-paste:**

```
Take [PRODUCT]. Find its 10 strongest competitors. Use each one where you can
(free tiers, demos, public pages) and record what it does well and badly.

Write an evaluation rubric from what real users of this category care about
(reviews, forums, support threads). Score my product and every competitor.

Then pick ONE dimension where my product could be clearly the best, given what I
already have. Explain why that one. Improve my product until it wins on that
dimension by the rubric, with before and after evidence. Open a PR. Do not ship.
```

**Notes**
- Ask for the reason behind the chosen dimension. A weak reason means a weak pick.
- Re-score against the same rubric after the change. No rubric change mid-run.

**Origin**
- From an AI assistant's answer to "how should I spend a large budget on my strongest
  model?" (November 2026). The ranking in that answer was its own guess.
- Limit: not yet run and measured by this repo. Open a PR with your result.
